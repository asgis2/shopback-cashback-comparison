## Why Cashback Sometimes "Disappears" (and How to Prevent It)

If ShopBack is legit, why do Reddit threads full of "my cashback didn't track" exist? Because tracking runs on the same machinery as every affiliate program on the internet: **last-click attribution**. Whichever referring source the retailer last sees in the cookie when checkout happens gets credit for the sale.

That means anything that overwrites ShopBack's click between you tapping "Shop Now" and you tapping "Place Order" can break the chain. The most common chain-breakers:

- **An older browser tab** that doesn't carry the new tracking tag.
- **A coupon extension** (Honey, RetailMeNot, etc.) that pops up mid-checkout and applies a code — it overwrites the affiliate cookie with its own, and now *it* gets the commission, not you.
- **A creator's affiliate link** clicked after the ShopBack click.
- **A browser ad-blocker** stripping affiliate URL parameters.
- **Switching to the retailer's app** mid-session, which loses the browser cookie entirely.

The fix is mostly habit, not settings:

1. **Start every session at ShopBack** — app, extension, or website. Don't skip and "start at the retailer this time."
2. **Click through using its "Shop Now" button** — that's the redirect that drops the tracking tag.
3. **Complete the purchase in the same session** — same browser, same tab where possible, no detours to other affiliate or coupon sources.
4. **Dismiss popup coupon extensions mid-checkout**, or disable them on retailers where cashback matters more than a coupon (often the cashback rate is higher than any coupon the extension would surface anyway).
5. **For high-value orders, use the ShopBack app** — it doesn't depend on browser cookies, so the risk of breakage is much lower.

If a purchase still doesn't track, file a missing-cashback claim via the ShopBack app at *Account → Cashback → Missing Cashback* within 30 days of the purchase date. After 30 days, the option disappears.
